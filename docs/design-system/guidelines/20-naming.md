# Naming

"Patente Pro" is a temporary name and a market-locked one: `patente` is the Italian word for a driving licence. The brief for a replacement: short, easy to say in Italian, English, Russian, German and Spanish, meaningful without being Italian-only, and free to grow into "the same app for another country".

## The landscape

Italy's category is crowded and its names are descriptive: Quiz Patente 2026 (several publishers), SuperQuizPatente, Drivoo, Patente Quiz World, and a translated edition called Quiz Patente Tradotto 2026. Abroad, the leaders are Zutobi (which runs country versions from one brand), the official DVSA Theory Test Kit in the UK, Lumo: Driving Theory Test, Theory Pass, Emyat, DMV Genie for the US, and a long tail of Führerschein apps in Germany. A name that is not "Quiz something" will stand out immediately.

## Shortlist

| Name | Says | Feels | Scales to other markets | Registrar check (28 Sep 2026) | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Passo** | "step" (IT, PT), "paso" (ES), "pass" (EN) | Warm, friendly, about progress and passing | Strong: works in five languages; "Passo UK", "Passo Deutschland" | passo.com and passo.app taken; trypasso.com and passocode.com free | Working name. Common word, so expect trademark neighbours |
| **Pronto** | "ready" (IT, PT); everyone knows it from phone calls | Confident, exam-ready | Strong | pronto.app and pronto.io taken; prontoroad.app, prontoroad.com and prontopass.app free | Runner-up. Crowded name; needs a qualifier |
| **Stradio** | strada (road) + studio | Distinctive, coined, easy to own | Fair: Italian-rooted, pronounceable everywhere | stradio.app free | Most ownable. Weaker story outside Italy |
| **Roadlingo** | "learn the language of the road" | Friendly, describes the Italian-for-the-exam angle | Strong (English), but reads as a language app | roadlingo.app free, .com taken | Good if the pitch is "for people learning Italian"; narrower otherwise |
| **Lanecraft** | the craft of driving | Neutral, English | Strong | lanecraft.app free | Safe but little emotional pull |

Registrar checks are a first pass at whether a domain can be bought today; they say nothing about trademarks or store listings.

## Ruled out

Lumo and Theory Pass already exist as driving-theory apps. Strada, Segno, Signa, Rotta, Viamo, Dritto, Tornante, Sosta, Semaforo and Cartello were unavailable as both `.com` and `.app`. Guida (`.app` and `.io`), Vai (`.app`), Verde (`.app`) and Vero (`.app`) were also taken, and Vero is a known social app; the two-way verdict Vero/Falso stays a feature word, not the product name.

## Recommendation

Use **Passo** as the working name (that is what the cover and wordmarks show), keep **Stradio** as the fallback if a trademark search turns up conflicts, and hold **Pronto** in reserve. Before committing to any of them:

1. Search EUIPO TMview and WIPO Global Brand Database for the name in classes 9 (software), 41 (education) and 42 (SaaS). This is not legal advice; a trademark attorney should confirm.
2. Search the Apple App Store and Google Play in Italian, English and Russian; check Instagram, TikTok and YouTube handles.
3. Say the name aloud in every launch language.
4. Decide the brand architecture: one master brand with a market suffix ("Passo Italia") or one listing per country. The design system supports either, since the Wordmark takes the name as a string.
