## A note on the underlying exam material

The questions originate from the theory quiz published by the Italian
Ministero delle Infrastrutture e dei Trasporti. Patente Pro is an independent
study aid: it is **not** an official publication and is not affiliated with,
endorsed by, or connected to any government body.

The MIT licence above covers the QuizPatenteB project. Whether the underlying
ministerial question texts carry their own protection is a separate question
of Italian law — Legge 633/1941 art. 5 excludes the texts of official acts of
the State from copyright, which is the usual basis for reproducing them, but
this is not legal advice. Before distributing commercially, confirm it with
someone qualified.
