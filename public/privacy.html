Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

The short version

Patente Pro has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics. Your study progress is stored on your own device and is never sent to us, because there is no server of ours to send it to.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your study progress — the questions you answered, your mistakes, mock-exam results, the chapters you read, your name if you typed one, and your settings. In the app installed from the store, this is kept in the app's own local storage on your phone. In the desktop version it is a file on your computer.
  • An offline cache of the questions, lessons and road-sign images, so the app works with no connection.

We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any of it. There is no advertising, no tracking, no third-party analytics and no advertising identifier.

Personal data

The app never asks for an email address, a phone number, a location or contacts. The name you may enter is only used to greet you on the dashboard, and it stays on your device with everything else.

Web hosting

The app's files are delivered by a hosting provider. Like any web server, it processes the technical information that is inherent to serving a request — such as your IP address and browser type — in its own server logs for security and reliability. We do not add analytics to those requests and we do not build profiles from them.

How this is enforced, not just promised

The app ships a Content-Security-Policy that permits network connections only to its own address. Even if someone found a way to inject code into the page, that code could not send anything anywhere. The app also declares that it will never use geolocation, the camera, the microphone or payment APIs, and the Android version requests no permissions at all and opts out of Android's cloud backup, so your data is never copied off the device in the background.

Children

The app is a study aid for a driving-licence exam and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Because the data never leaves your device, you are always in control of it:

  • In the app: Settings → Reset all progress erases everything.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its local storage with it.
  • Settings → Backup exports a copy of your progress as a file, if you want to keep or move it yourself.

About the exam content

The questions and lessons are study material. They are not an official publication of any government body, and passing practice exams in this app does not guarantee passing the real one.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the contact address listed on the app's store page.
